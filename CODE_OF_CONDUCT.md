# Code of Conduct

We want Octomynd Maestro to be a welcoming, constructive open-source project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, private paths, user data, or private conversations.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing
secrets, and any other conduct that would make participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately through the channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports
will be reviewed by the maintainers and handled as confidentially as practical. A maintainer may
remove content, restrict participation, or take other action needed to protect the community.
